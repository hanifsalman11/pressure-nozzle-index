Frequently Asked Questions (Dewalt Pressure Washer 2100 Psi)

What size nozzle orifice do I need for the Dewalt Pressure Washer 2100 Psi?

To operate at the factory rating of 2100 PSI and 2.5 GPM, you must install an exact #3.5 orifice nozzle. Installing an undersized tip like a #3.0 will spike pressure and force the unloader into constant bypass, while stepping up to a #5.0 or #6.0 orifice reduces dynamic pressure to approximately 700–1000 PSI for safe wood and delicate-surface rinsing.

What size surface cleaner can the Dewalt Pressure Washer 2100 Psi run?

The machine reliably powers a 10-inch rotary surface cleaner based on the PWNA-certified guideline of 4 inches of deck diameter per 1 GPM of pump delivery. The spray bar requires two 1/8-inch NPT MEG 25018 nozzles (25-degree spray angle with a #1.8 orifice each) to divide the 2.5 GPM output evenly and retain 2100 PSI.

What hose diameter should I use for 100ft runs on the Dewalt Pressure Washer 2100 Psi?

Use a 3/8-inch inner-diameter steel-braided hose. Pumping 2.5 GPM through 100 feet of standard 1/4-inch hose generates approximately 120–150 PSI of friction loss and excessive fluid turbulence, whereas a 3/8-inch hose drops friction loss to less than 25–30 PSI, delivering maximum pressure and volume directly to the cleaning tool.
