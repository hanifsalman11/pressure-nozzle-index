Frequently Asked Questions (Simpson Powershot Pressure Washer)

What size nozzle orifice do I need for the Simpson Powershot Pressure Washer (4,000 PSI / 4.0 GPM)?

Using the Bernoulli orifice equation (Orifice = 4.0 GPM × √(4000 ÷ 4000 PSI)), your exact single-wand nozzle size is #4.0 (e.g., 15040, 25040, and 40040 tips). To lower pressure to 2,500 PSI for wood decks or car washing, step up to a #5.1 orifice.

What is the best surface cleaner size for 4.0 GPM at 4,000 PSI?

Multiply your GPM (4.0) by 4 inches: a 16-inch rotary surface cleaner is the ideal match. It should be equipped with two #2.00 (25020) 1/4-inch NPT threaded nozzles.

How much PSI do I lose through 100 feet of pressure washer hose at 4.0 GPM?

At 4.0 GPM, a 100-foot 3/8-inch ID hose loses only ~112 PSI to friction, whereas a narrow 1/4-inch ID hose loses over 380 PSI. Always run 3/8-inch steel-braided hose for runs over 50 feet.
